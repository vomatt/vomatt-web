# Terms of Service and Privacy Policy: corrections (draft)

> **What this is.** Paste-ready edits to the published Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
> (`pGeneral` documents `terms-of-service` and `privacy-policy`, English and 繁體中文, edited in
> vomatt-admin's Studio). They make the pages match what vomatt-web and vomatt-api do today.
> Written against the pages as published on 2026-11-27 (Privacy Policy dated November 26, Terms
> dated November 7).
>
> **Out of scope.** Pro, paid plans, phone numbers and result reports: see
> `monetization-pages-draft.md`. The fuller reporting, appeals and copyright pages: see
> `launch-pages-draft.md` (not published yet).
>
> **How to read it.** Each edit says where it goes, then gives the English and the 繁體中文 text.
> "Replace" swaps the whole paragraph or list item named; "Add" inserts a new one.
>
> **Placeholders.** Anything only the owner can fill is marked `[OWNER: ...]`. Remove the notes
> before publishing, and update "Last updated" on each page.
>
> **Have a Taiwan lawyer review before publishing**, in particular: the operator details (PDPA
> Art. 8), the copyright wording in T5 (Copyright Act Chapter VI-1, including the
> three-infringement rule in Art. 90-4), and whether always-on visit measurement (P5) needs
> consent if members in the EU are served.

## Part 3. Sign-up line in Chinese

`pSignUp.policyMessage` has one language, so Chinese users see the English line under the email
field. Showing a Chinese line needs a second field in vomatt-admin (as `gAnnouncement` has
`messageZh`) and a small change in `AuthFlow.tsx`. The text:

> 註冊即表示你同意[服務條款](/terms-of-service)與[隱私權政策](/privacy-policy)。
