## Caption · English

```
You've been in this room.

Fourteen people, three of them talk, and the decision goes down as unanimous. Being heard and being counted turned out to be two different things. Meetings give you the first. Online polls give you the second. We wanted both in one place.

So we named it after both. Voice matters. Vote matters. vomatt.

On vomatt:
· Anyone can ask, and anyone it concerns can say why.
· One person, one vote, sealed while the poll is open.
· When it closes, every voter gets the result at the same moment.

And on purpose: no live counts, no streaks, no ranking people by followers.

What would you put to a vote? Your street, your team, your dinner table. Tell us below and we'll open the best ones as public polls.

Every voice, counted. Link in bio.

#vomatt #voicematters #votematters #everyvoicecounted #civictech #community #每個聲音都算數
```

## Posting checklist

- **Before posting:** bio reads "Voice matters. Vote matters. Every voice, counted." with the link.
- **Post:** carousel of seven, caption above, alt text on each slide.
- **Pin** the post to the top of the grid.
- **First comment:** the caption in the other language.
- **Story, same day:** slide 1 with a link sticker, then slide 7 with a question sticker "What would you put to a vote?".
- **Replies:** answer every comment within a day. A suggested question gets "Good one. Opening it this week."
- **Follow through:** open the two or three best questions as Public polls and post each as a Question of the week. This is the promise on slide 7.
- **Post one:** keep it. Add one line to its caption: "vomatt = voice matters + vote matters."

## Check before it goes out

- Slide 7 promises to open the best questions as public polls. Only post it if someone will do that within the week.
- Slides 3 to 5 show product screens. They match the app as of the 29 October build; re-check if the ballot or results design changes before posting.
- The signature reads "the team at vomatt". Swap in a founder's name if you want a personal note.
