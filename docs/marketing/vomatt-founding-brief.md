# vomatt · founding brief

Copy for the about page and the homepage hero of the marketing site (about.vomatt.com), plus the caption for the founding Instagram post. Written in the product's own register: plain, calm, no hype.

## The name

**vomatt** = **vo**ice **matt**ers + **vo**te **matt**ers.

Two words we kept, and everything we cut to make room for them. Say it "voh-mat".

## One line

Every voice, counted.

## Boilerplate (48 words)

vomatt is a public voting platform where anyone can ask a question and anyone can answer it. Ballots stay sealed while a poll is open, so nobody votes with the crowd. When it closes, the result goes back to everyone who voted. One person, one vote. Voice matters. Vote matters.

## About page

### Why we built it

In a group chat, a council hall or a team meeting, the decision usually goes to whoever talks most. Everyone else gets counted as silence. We wanted a place where the quiet answer weighs exactly as much as the loud one.

So we built the simplest thing that does that: a question, a few options, a deadline. Anyone can ask. Anyone it concerns can answer. Nobody can shout.

### What the name means

vomatt is two ideas folded into one word. Voice matters: everyone gets to say what they think. Vote matters: what they say is counted, once, and the count is honoured. We kept the two words and cut everything else.

### How vomatt counts

- **Sealed while open.** While a poll runs you see how many people voted, never the running count. Nobody votes with the crowd, and nobody is nudged by it.
- **One person, one vote.** You can change or withdraw your vote until the poll closes. After that, it stands.
- **The result goes back to everyone.** When a poll closes, every voter sees the same outcome at the same moment. Nobody waits for a summary from whoever asked.
- **Your name, your call.** Hide your name from the voter list on any poll. Your profile never lists what you voted for.

### Who it's for

Residents before a council consultation. A shop association picking a market night. A team weighing a policy. A class, a building, a family, a group chat picking dinner. If more than two people have to live with the answer, it's a vomatt question.

### What we won't do

No live counts that invite herd voting. No streaks, no confetti, no leaderboards. No ranking of people by followers. Turnout is celebrated, popularity is not.

## 繁體中文

**名字的由來**：vomatt 是 **vo**ice **matt**ers（聲音算數）加上 **vo**te **matt**ers（一票算數）。兩個詞留下，其餘都刪掉。

**一句話**：每個聲音，都算數。

**首頁標題**：每個聲音，都算數。
**副標**：問一個清楚的問題，讓每個人有同樣的一票，投票結束時一起看結果。
**按鈕**：發起投票

**三條規則**
- 投票結束前，票數保持封存。
- 一人一票。
- 結果回到每一位投票的人手上。

**為什麼做 vomatt**：在群組、里民大會或團隊會議裡，決定往往落在說話最多的人身上，其他人被當成沉默。我們想要一個地方，讓安靜的答案和大聲的答案一樣重。所以做了最簡單的東西：一個問題、幾個選項、一個期限。任何人都能問，相關的人都能答，沒有人能用音量取勝。
